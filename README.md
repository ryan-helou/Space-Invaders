You fly a ship along the bottom of the screen and shoot the rows of alien invaders above you while dodging the shots they drop. There are three levels, and each one adds more enemies who fire more often. I built it in Java, with its own sound effects, explosions and arcade-style lettering.

## What you can do with it

- Move your ship with the W, A, S and D keys and fire with the space bar.
- Play through three levels. Your weapon gets stronger each level: one shot, then two angled shots, then three.
- Survive with three lives. The screen shows your lives, the level and how long you've been playing.
- Press N to change how your missiles look.
